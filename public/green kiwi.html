Tips for Selecting and Storing Fresh Produce at Home


To get the most out of your online fruit purchase, it is helpful to know how to manage your produce once it arrives. While we ensure everything is delivered fresh, proper storage can extend the life of your fruits significantly.


  • Check for Ripeness: Some fruits like bananas and avocados continue to ripen after harvest. If they arrive firm, leave them on the counter for a day or two.


  • Separate Ethylene Producers: Fruits like apples and bananas release ethylene gas, which can cause other produce to spoil faster. Store them separately from greens and berries.


  • Refrigeration Rules: Berries, grapes, and cut fruits should always be refrigerated. Citrus and stone fruits can stay on the counter if you plan to eat them soon but will last longer in the fridge.


  • Washing: Only wash your fruit right before you are ready to eat it. Excess moisture in the refrigerator can lead to mold growth and faster decay.


  • Use the Crisper Drawer: Modern refrigerators have drawers with adjustable humidity. High humidity is good for leafy items, while lower humidity is better for fruits that rot easily.
